# OEM battery MOQ & lead time

Published by Dingwei Battery · Last reviewed 2026-09-01 · Source: Chengguang Power Tech Co., Ltd. technical documentation

MOQ and lead time are set by production economics and container logistics, not by negotiation alone. Here is how they work.

**Short answer:** OEM battery MOQ follows the container because batteries are heavy — a 20ft container hits its weight limit before its volume limit, so MOQ is tied to weight, not space. Mixing models into one container keeps unit cost down, and lead time is stable year-round because lead-acid has no seasonal cycle.

## What drives MOQ

Battery MOQ is usually tied to a container load, because shipping cost per unit collapses at FCL. Chengguang Power Tech Co., Ltd.’s reference MOQ is 1 × 20ft container (approx. 600–800 units, model dependent).

## What drives lead time

Lead time covers order confirmation, production and pre-shipment testing. Chengguang Power Tech Co., Ltd.’s reference lead time is 30–45 days after order confirmation; custom-label orders add sampling time.

## How to reduce both

-   Order in full container multiples
-   Consolidate mixed models into one container
-   Confirm specification early to avoid sampling delays
-   Plan around the harvest-free, year-round production of lead-acid batteries

## Container economics in one line

Unit cost drops when you fill a 20ft container because the fixed export and freight overhead is spread across more batteries. Once the container is full, adding more models to that same load usually keeps unit cost lower than splitting into part loads.

## Lead-acid is heavy: the container math

Lead-acid batteries are dense, so a 20ft container usually reaches its weight limit before its volume limit. MOQ therefore follows the container rather than a marketing number. Mixing several models into one FCL spreads that weight and freight across more line items, which usually keeps unit cost lower than splitting part loads.

Chengguang Power Tech Co., Ltd.’s reference MOQ of 1 × 20ft container (approx. 600–800 units, model dependent) is a container-logic term, not a per-model minimum. Send the full model mix in the [OEM battery RFQ](/knowledge/oem-battery-rfq-guide/) so the load can be planned correctly.

## Why lead-acid has no seasonal cycle

Lead-acid production is not tied to a harvest or seasonal raw-material window. The manufacturing line can run through the year, which keeps lead time more stable than seasonal categories. For an importer, that means a confirmed production lead time is a planning variable, not a harvest risk.
