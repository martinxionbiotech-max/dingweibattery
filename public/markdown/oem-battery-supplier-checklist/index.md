# OEM battery supplier checklist

Published by Dingwei Battery · Last reviewed 2026-09-10 · Source: Chengguang Power Tech Co., Ltd. technical documentation

Run a supplier through these checks before you commit to an OEM battery order. The checklist is a filter: it removes the candidates that would waste a factory audit, but it does not replace the audit itself.

**Short answer:** The red flag that should stop you immediately is a quote given before the standard family and CCA basis are fixed. Use a 24-point checklist across five groups — credentials, capacity, testing, lead time and after-sales — and treat a supplier that fails 3 or more checks as unqualified. A checklist is a filter, not a guarantee: it catches weak suppliers but does not replace sampling and change control.

## How to use this checklist

Work top to bottom. The five groups are ordered by the cost of getting each one wrong: a credential failure is the cheapest to catch and the most expensive to discover late, so it comes first. Mark each item pass or fail, and stop the process if any single item in the credentials group fails — the rest of the list is moot if the entity is not what it claims.

For the verification method behind the credentials group, see the [supplier verification](/knowledge/battery-supplier-verification/) guide.

## Group 1 — Credentials (qualification)

| # | Check | Pass signal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Business licence with unified social credit code | Matches gsxt.gov.cn; scope is production, not trading |
| 2 | Registered capital | Consistent with running a production line |
| 3 | IATF 16949 certificate (automotive supply) | Number verifiable with the certifier; within a 3-year cycle |
| 4 | ISO 9001 certificate | Current, and scope actually covers batteries |
| 5 | Destination-market certifications (CE / SASO / etc.) | Issued by a recognised body for your market |

## Group 2 — Capacity

| # | Check | Pass signal |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | Factory area and production lines | Stated and consistent with the audit |
| 7 | Daily output vs your annual volume | Absorbs your volume without making you the smallest customer |
| 8 | Casting capacity | Stated and believable against equipment |
| 9 | Formation capacity | The real bottleneck; consistent with the output claim |
| 10 | Export experience and destinations | Names markets they serve and can document them |

Chengguang Power Tech Co., Ltd. operates a 200,000 m² plant with 18 automated lines producing 40,000 batteries per day and exporting to 70+ countries.

## Group 3 — Testing

| # | Check | Pass signal |
|---|---|---|
| 11 | CCA test method named | JIS −15°C vs EN/SAE −18°C stated, not “CCA” alone |
| 12 | 100% end-of-line electrical test | Confirmed as a line step, not a sample |
| 13 | Capacity (C20) verification | Tested to 10.5 V at the 20-hour rate |
| 14 | Incoming material inspection | Lead alloy and separator checked on receipt |
| 15 | Batch traceability | A batch maps back to raw materials and date |

## Group 4 — Lead time

| # | Check | Pass signal |
|---|---|---|
| 16 | Production lead time | Stated as a breakdown, not a single number |
| 17 | Sampling lead time | Separated from mass-production lead time |
| 18 | Pre-shipment inspection window | Included in the schedule, not added later |
| 19 | MOQ logic | Explained in container terms, not a bare number |

## Group 5 — After-sales & terms

| # | Check | Pass signal |
|---|---|---|
| 20 | Warranty duration | 12 months from bill of lading date, from bill of lading date |
| 21 | Warranty exclusions written | Narrow, explicit, not “broad exclusions apply” |
| 22 | Claim handling process | Defined inspection and settlement steps |
| 23 | Change control (ECO) | A written process, not a verbal promise |
| 24 | Payment and Incoterms | T/T (30% deposit, 70% before shipment) or L/C at sight; FOB Tianjin / CFR / CIF |

## Scoring the result

Count the failures. Zero to two failures across all five groups and you have a candidate worth auditing. Three or more failures, or any failure in the credentials group, and the candidate is unqualified — remove it before you spend an audit on it. The threshold is deliberately strict on credentials because a supplier that is not what it claims cannot be fixed by a better price.

## Red flags

-   No current certificates when you ask for them, or no certificate numbers.
-   Quoting before the standard family and CCA basis are fixed.
-   No sample available from a recent production run.
-   A capacity claim the formation area cannot support.
-   A warranty with exclusions so broad the coverage is effectively empty.

If any of these appear, slow the process down and re-check the [five-point decision framework](/knowledge/how-choose-oem-battery-manufacturer/) before placing volume. Dingwei Battery provides OEM and private-label battery programs manufactured by Chengguang Power Tech Co., Ltd. (founded 2002).

## Check the quote, not just the price

A quote is a six-part scope. If any part is missing, the number is not comparable:

-   **Tooling costs** — one-time or phased, and refundable or not.
-   **MOQ structure** — prototype / pilot / production clearly separated.
-   **Test report scope** — transport, safety and performance reports listed.
-   **Warranty terms** — duration, exclusions and failure handling.
-   **Engineering support** — included or separately charged.
-   **Change control** — a formal ECO process, not a verbal agreement.

Use the same six fields when you issue the [OEM battery RFQ](/knowledge/oem-battery-rfq-guide/) so suppliers are forced to quote on scope, not on a bare unit number.
