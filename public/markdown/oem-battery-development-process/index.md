# The OEM battery development process

Published by Dingwei Battery · Last reviewed 2026-09-01 · Source: Chengguang Power Tech Co., Ltd. technical documentation

OEM development is a gated process. Understanding the stages tells you where to be involved and where to insist on sign-off.

**Short answer:** OEM battery development moves through consultation, specification, design and branding, sampling, mass production, quality inspection, and shipping. Buyers should be most involved at specification, sampling, inspection and shipment, because those are the gates that prevent specification drift.

## The seven stages

1.  Consultation
2.  Specification
3.  Design & branding
4.  Sampling
5.  Mass production
6.  Quality inspection
7.  Logistics & shipping

## Where buyers should be involved

-   Specification — sign off every parameter
-   Sampling — test before approving
-   Quality inspection — agree the acceptance criteria
-   Shipment — confirm documentation

## Why gates matter

Each gate is a decision point. Skipping a gate is how a specification drifts and a repeat order comes back different.

## Stage-by-stage: who does what

| Stage | Manufacturer delivers | Buyer action |
|---|---|---|
| 1\. Consultation | Feasibility view on the requested specification | Confirm vehicle, market and volume intent |
| 2\. Specification | Written spec sheet with every parameter | Sign every line, including the CCA test basis |
| 3\. Design & branding | Label and packaging artwork | Approve artwork and documentation |
| 4\. Sampling | Pre-production sample from a production-equivalent line | Test before approving; record results |
| 5\. Mass production | Scheduled production with in-process control | Confirm the schedule and any change freeze |
| 6\. Quality inspection | Inspection and test data | Agree acceptance criteria before release |
| 7\. Shipping | Export packaging and documents | Confirm documents before shipment |

## Where the buyer must sign off

Four gates need a buyer signature, not just a manufacturer confirmation:

1.  **Specification** — sign every parameter, including the CCA test basis.
2.  **Sample** — test the pre-production sample before approval.
3.  **Quality standard** — agree acceptance criteria before mass production.
4.  **Shipment** — confirm the documents before release.

Skip one gate and the next order can drift from the approved sample. Keep the sign-off sequence tied to the [OEM program phases](/knowledge/how-oem-battery-programs-work/) so both sides know where approval is required.
