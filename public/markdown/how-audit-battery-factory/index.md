# How to audit a lead-acid battery factory

Published by Dingwei Battery · Last reviewed 2026-09-01 · Source: Chengguang Power Tech Co., Ltd. technical documentation

A factory audit is how you verify that a battery manufacturer can deliver your specification repeatably — not just once. Run these checks before committing volume.

**Short answer:** Audit a battery factory against five verifiable areas — certifications, capacity, quality control, traceability and export documents. The most telling single question is curing time: a factory that rushes curing to save days produces weak plates that fail early, so ask for the curing window, not just the capacity numbers.

## Certifications

Request current certificates, not claims. Chengguang Power Tech Co., Ltd. holds IATF 16949, ISO 9001, ISO 45001, OHSAS 18001.

## Production capability

Check factory area, number of production lines and daily output against your annual volume. Chengguang Power Tech Co., Ltd. operates a 200,000 m² plant with 18 automated lines producing 40,000 batteries per day.

## Quality control & testing

-   Incoming material inspection
-   In-process checks (grid casting, pasting, curing, formation)
-   100% end-of-line electrical test
-   Batch traceability
-   Lab testing for CCA and capacity

## Export readiness

Confirm export experience, documentation capability and destination markets. Chengguang Power Tech Co., Ltd. exports to 70+ countries.

## Audit scorecard

-   **Certification** — current certificates are visible and verifiable.
-   **Capacity** — plant area, lines and output map to your annual volume.
-   **Quality** — in-process and end-of-line tests are documented.
-   **Export** — destination markets and document capability are clear.

Use the same four columns for every factory, then compare against the [Dingwei factory overview](/factory/) as a worked example.

## What to bring home from an audit

-   Current certificates
-   Sample of a recent production run
-   A specification sheet for your reference model
-   Agreed commercial terms

## The ten stages to audit

Walk the factory in the order the battery is made. For each stage, ask what is measured and how rejects are controlled.

1.  **Grid casting** — check grid thickness and alloy consistency.
2.  **Paste mixing** — check mix control and batch records.
3.  **Pasting** — check plate weight and paste application.
4.  **Curing** — check time and conditions; a typical window is 2–4 days.
5.  **Assembly** — check plate alignment and separator handling.
6.  **Welding** — check inter-cell and terminal welds.
7.  **Electrolyte filling** — check acid volume and density control.
8.  **Formation charging** — check the charging profile and duration.
9.  **Sealing** — check lid, vent and leak control.
10.  **Testing** — confirm 100% end-of-line electrical test plus CCA and capacity sampling.

Take this sequence with you, and compare it to the documented checks on the [OEM battery supplier checklist](/knowledge/oem-battery-supplier-checklist/).

## Lead-acid audit is simpler than lithium

Lead-acid audit effort concentrates on process consistency and raw materials. A lithium-cell audit adds cell safety and thermal-runaway controls; lead-acid does not carry the same cell-safety audit burden. That makes the lead-acid audit shorter and easier to standardize for OEM buyers.
